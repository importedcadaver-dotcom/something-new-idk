## What project are we working on?

**Acorn (“Muffin”) HoneyDweller** is a wealthy six-month-old male brown bear cub who really plays **Fortnite** and runs his own gaming/lifestyle channel in a fictional bear-dominated world with a bear-only internet. His toddler-like emotions, unusual early reading, genuine channel independence and family are meant to drive meaningful consequences. Acorn's mother **Mama** is home-oriented/non-gamer; **Mommy** plays games and leads the legitimate family corporation; twin **Millie** competes with him, older brother **Rudy** is delinquent and idolized, and grandmother **Cynthia** (working name) hides a criminal organization. His mothers, Grandma and siblings have different knowledge, motivations and conflicts.

**Current physical signature:** Very chubby fluffy brown bear, hazel eyes, near-white belly, deep reddish-cinnamon **anatomical RIGHT ear**, lighter **anatomical FRONT LEFT paw sock**, large paws and naturalistic mostly quadrupedal body. Stream overlay's middle-left webcam position is **on-screen**, not a real room camera mount. Q57 real-game research references are not all Acorn-played games.

**One established later viral incident:** Acorn deliberately fouled a donated food pile on-stream to resist viewer pressure, received a platform warning and later felt embarrassed. Concurrency before **500–2,000**, after **5,000–20,000**; these are not follower counts. No sponsors during that incident. Do not invent further first-stream incidents without permission.
