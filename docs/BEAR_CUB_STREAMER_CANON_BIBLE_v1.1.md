## 1. Scope, premise and tone

**Premise — APPROVED (H/R):** Acorn "Muffin" HoneyDweller is a six-month-old male brown bear cub from an extraordinarily wealthy bear dynasty. He has his own famous livestream gaming channel, plays actual video games including Fortnite, and governs the channel on his terms. Family wealth makes technical production, toys, gaming equipment and luxury available without requiring sponsorship income. His family contains two loving but overindulgent lesbian mothers, a mischievous twin sister, a delinquent older brother, his polar-bear girlfriend, and an intimidating grandmother who secretly has international criminal interests.

**Three coexisting perspectives — APPROVED Q1 (H):** (1) adorable, absurd domestic/cub comedy; (2) satirical treatment of wealth, privilege and celebrity; (3) serious dystopian bear-dominated society. Perspective determines tone; the world is not forced into one register. The existence of lethal oppression and organized crime does not mean every family breakfast is a crime thriller; cute protagonists do not sanitize the world's cruelty.

**Working project name:** Bear Cub Streamer / Bear Cub Twitch Streamer. A permanent franchise or channel title has not been explicitly approved.

## 2. Species biology and bear civilization

### 2.1 Bears' bodies and technology — APPROVED (Q4 H; later R)

Intelligent bears preserve **realistic, naturalistic bear anatomy**: predominantly quadrupedal posture, true muzzles, bear paws and claws, recognizable species forms, and not generic human-shaped anthro bodies. They have **enhanced paw dexterity**, enough to operate sophisticated **bear-designed/adapted** controls. Complex technology, furniture, rooms and tools must be usable through paws, muzzles, grips, body positioning and specialized equipment. This is an evolved fictional difference and an engineering adaptation, not automatic bipedal human hands.

**UNRESOLVED:** How fine manipulation works; practical reach and strength constraints, specialized controller/keyboard layouts, posture duration, medical/toileting arrangements, cub-sized interfaces.

### 2.2 Age equivalence and lifespan — APPROVED (Q2–3 H/R)

**1 actual bear year ≈ 4 human years developmentally/cognitively/emotionally.** This is not a universal multiplier for physical age or lifespan. Bears may live **80–120 actual years**. A six-month-old cub such as Acorn/Millie has roughly a human two-year-old developmental equivalent, with individuality and exceptional proficiencies.

The setting needs **separate curves** for bodily growth, cognitive development, schooling, sexuality/maturity, adulthood, senescence and lifespan. The absence of such curves is an **unresolved modeling dependency**, not permission to overwrite the approved 4:1 rule.

### 2.3 Species, reproduction, ordinary animals — APPROVED / PARTIAL (R)

The central HoneyDweller family is **brown bears**. Rudy's girlfriend is a **polar bear**; brown/polar partnering is possible. Real brown/polar interbreeding was discussed as a biological reference, but specific fantasy reproduction/inheritance rules remain open. Different species can have cultural/status associations without every individual embodying a stereotype.

Polar bears in this civilization have cultural associations with relatively elevated/upper-middle-class status, **not an invariable biological socioeconomic rank**. Rudy's polar girlfriend's family is comparatively poor for its circumstances despite decent parental wages and approximately thirty children.

The world includes **nonsapient** normal animals: domestic **cats, kittens and red pandas** may be kept as pets. They are not sapient bear citizens. Specific HoneyDweller pets are **not yet selected**.

Fantasy polar-bear reproduction may be more prolific than real-world polar bear reproduction; the exact approved exception concerns Rudy's girlfriend's mother having unusually large/multiple litters over decades. No exact physiological schedule is approved.

## 3. Humans, labor, institutions and class

### 3.1 Permanent bear dominance — APPROVED (R)

Bears dominate humans **permanently** at a civilizational level. Humans produce virtually all bear possessions, electronics and technology via manufacturing/factory labor under bear control. Individual resistance, uprisings, personal survival and limited changes remain possible; a **major lasting successful human revolution ending bear dominance is excluded**.

Many bears regard humans as dirty/inferior, but fear their **intelligence, invention and creativity**, not chiefly their bodily strength. Past incidents also contribute to that fear; their historical particulars are not yet established.

Wealthy bear families view a human anywhere near their cubs as scandalous. Poorer bear families may have direct human employees. HoneyDweller household service and direct cub-contact staff are **bears**; human industrial labor in the wider supply chain remains compatible.

### 3.2 Bear-only internet — APPROVED (R)

Humans cannot legally access the bear internet in ordinary circumstances. Unauthorized human access is **a capital crime**, potentially resulting in execution and being eaten by bears. The bear-only network includes identity verification, surveillance, social status hierarchy, propaganda, and harsh consequences for rule-breaking. The ban also rules out casual online human/bear game friendships.

**UNRESOLVED:** Manufacturing/testing connectivity boundaries, how human workers build network devices without obtaining unauthorized access, enforcement technology, human living conditions, specific government forms, exceptions if any, geographic polities and laws. Do not invent a human resistance victory or online human accounts to fill narrative gaps.

### 3.3 Institutions and labor — OPEN

Bear governments, enforcement, class and species hierarchies, trade, industrial facilities, media/celebrity industries and international territories exist, but specific nations, population percentages, currencies, school curricula and tax laws have **not been established in this universe**. Don't import bear-species census figures or economic systems from unrelated game projects.

The HoneyDwellers' legitimate defense/security contracts with the bear government are approved (Q35). **Which contracts, if any, are directly tied to human oppression is undecided.** No universal contract fraud has been established.

## 5. Acorn ("Muffin") HoneyDweller — lead character

### 5.1 Identity — APPROVED (Q2 H, Q29 D, earlier R)
- Six-month-old **male brown bear**.
- Official first name: **Acorn**; family surname: **HoneyDweller**.
- Approved affectionate family nickname: **Muffin**.
- Dark red is his favorite color, not necessarily the channel's only color.
- Family's extremely wealthy youngest son and child celebrity streamer.
- Earlier placeholder **Berry** is SUPERSEDED. Waffle, Pockets, Chestnut, Kit, Reed and Dewey were naming contenders, not the approved first name.

### 5.2 Personality — APPROVED (R)

**Spoiled and demanding**, accustomed to luxury and indulgence. **Clingy** toward his moms, affectionate/sweet, praise-seeking, talkative when safe, asks for active feedback. **Emotionally sensitive**, prone to tears, tantrums, hurt feelings and giving up under emotional overload rather than demonstrating unlimited persistence. Competitive in games and upset by losses. **Shy near strangers** but outgoing, performative, confident and chaotic at home/on stream. Curious, mischievous, can disobey; admires and increasingly imitates delinquent brother Rudy.

He genuinely wishes Millie would treat him more kindly; her name-calling hurts him. Near Grandma he becomes quieter, vigilant, uncomfortable and afraid.

### 5.3 Physical play and care — APPROVED (R)

Likes **tag, running, climbing, swimming**, occasional outdoor adventures and dirt. Mostly prefers staying indoors and dislikes bright/hot sun. Often climbs somewhere high, gets stuck, cries, cannot work out how to descend and may resist or complicate rescue.

Afraid of **snakes and centipedes**. Bath acceptance requires a **temperature he finds just right**; otherwise he may flee, refuse, cry or bite caretakers. Resists toothbrushing. These traits should manifest as consistent responses rather than arbitrary randomness.

### 5.4 Language and cognition — APPROVED Q24 (R)

Speaks in short, simple, imperfect toddler sentences, repeats phrases, struggles with some pronunciation, frequently narrates his activity and orders/request things directly. Gaming vocabulary is unusually sophisticated. **Exceptional early reading** for his developmental equivalent is approved: do NOT downgrade to poor/limited reading. Nevertheless, fast moving livestream chat and donations require **text-to-speech**. Reading is exceptional; broader emotional/social judgment is not adult-level.

### 5.5 Channel agency — APPROVED (R)

Acorn **owns and controls his channel**; his word is law in **ordinary channel decisions**. He may go live without permission, select content, ban/unban, make moderation rules, reject gifts, ask others to leave, and sometimes even evict Audrey from participation because he wants to stream solo. Bear production staff technically operate/assist as needed; they are not the ultimate ordinary owners or bosses. **Emergency caregiver and safety overrides are not designed yet** and must not silently erase his ordinary authority.
