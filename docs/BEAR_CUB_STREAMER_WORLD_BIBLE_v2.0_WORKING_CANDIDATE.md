## 2. Bear biology and chronological development

**H/R — Early-development baseline:** During development, **one actual bear year corresponds roughly to four human developmental years** (cognitive/emotional approximation). A six-month cub is approximately two human developmental years old as a broad comparison. Bears in this fantasy setting have **80–120 actual-year lifespans**; the 4:1 early-development analogy is **NOT a fixed conversion applied to the entire lifespan**. [Prior bible §2](BEAR_CUB_STREAMER_CANON_BIBLE_v1.1.1.md).

**D — Q44 E:** Young cubs look and grow **broadly naturalistically at first**; later physical development, puberty, nursing/weaning, body completion and longevity may use independently modeled rules.
