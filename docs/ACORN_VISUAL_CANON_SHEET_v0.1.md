## Character identity and silhouette

Acorn (“Muffin”) HoneyDweller is a **six-month-old male brown bear cub**. He must retain naturalistic bear morphology, a predominantly **quadrupedal posture**, bear muzzle, ears, paws and claws. He is exceptionally **chubby/fat and visibly well fed**, with a large rounded soft baby face, full cheeks, dense thick fluffy coat, short stout legs, an **extra-wide tail**, **very large/relatively long ears with a slight droop**, and **abnormally enormous paws** (larger than his same-age twin Millie's). His physical traits are stylized in *proportion* by owner approval but must not be converted into humanoid anatomy or a stuffed toy.

No exact weight, body length, paw ratio, tail length or ear droop angle has been chosen.

## Eyes and facial expression

**Hazel irises** are approved. Eyes and ears should communicate emotions clearly within naturalistic bear anatomy. Eye shape, exact iris hue distribution, markings around eyes, exact pupil shape, nose color and expression pose inventory are OPEN. No cartoon human irises or anthropomorphic eyelids are authorized by these choices.

## Approved signature features

- A **lighter fur sock on the front left paw**.
- **One deep almost-red cinnamon ear**; side OPEN.
- The broad white belly is an approved coat region, but was not designated as an additional named signature mark.
- No other distinctive facial/forehead/eyebrow/cheek/chest/body markings are selected. Other candidate markings can be chosen in a later interview.

## Continuity and negative constraints

**Must preserve:** Very plump juvenile brown bear, floppy-ish large bear ears, oversized left-sock paw, extra-wide tail, dark body/back, white belly, cinnamon second tone including single red-cinnamon ear, hazel eyes, naturalistic quadruped.

**Must not assume:** Specific ear side, white sock instead of merely lighter, matching paw socks, a matching red ear, beige instead of white belly, cinnamon replacing dark body/back, a long fox/cat tail, rabbit ears, human hands, explicit eye shape, or individual marks other than those chosen.
