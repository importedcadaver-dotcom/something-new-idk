## Specific risk issues to address without retconning

**H2 feasibility:** It is canon that Rudy and his unnamed polar-bear girlfriend did most of the excavation/build, Acorn participated, and both parents are oblivious. It is *not canon* how excavation, spoil, equipment, noise, structural support, power, ventilation or security escaped notice in a staffed wealthy estate. The automatic paw-pressure hatch, broad lever (usable by all three), quiet lift, shallow stairs and personal under-bed gap are conceptual, not safety certification.

**Acorn's autonomy:** Acorn controls ordinary broadcast status, guests, bans and refusing requests; caregivers have genuine care/emergency responsibilities. Specific triggers for intervention are **unselected**. Don't invent adult-level judgment for a cub or erase his autonomy under generic parental ownership.

**Platform and world:** Literal current-world Twitch rules vs a fictional bear platform remains **OPEN**; the older combined audit cautions against literal underage service assumptions. Real Fortnite gameplay plus bear-only internet demands a future explicit fictional technology/distribution model.

**Hidden knowledge:** H1 parent-known; H2 parent-unknown. Other family/staff awareness is mostly unconfirmed, not “definitely absent.” Mother's entering Acorn's room does **not automatically prove** she sees or discovers the hidden basement. No canonical scripted apology, lie, punishment or discovery has been approved.

**Historical evidence:** Q6–17 original answer transcripts are incompletely recovered. First names Rosa/Rosalind, Audrey and Cynthia are working where specified; girlfriend unnamed; no first-stream chronology invented.
