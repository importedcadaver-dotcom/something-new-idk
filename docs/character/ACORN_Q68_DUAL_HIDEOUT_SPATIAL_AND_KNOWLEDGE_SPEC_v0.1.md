## Established locations

**Hideout H1 — Hallway bookcase retreat.** Parents **had this retreat built for Acorn**. Access is concealed **behind a secret bookshelf door**. Its hallway entrance is **directly opposite the gaming/streaming studio**, within the same short private corridor that independently connects the sleeping room, playroom and studio (Q67). Acorn's moms **know it exists**.

**Hideout H2 — Sleeping-room secret.** Acorn, older brother **Rudy**, and Rudy's **unnamed polar-bear girlfriend** jointly built a second retreat **inside Acorn's sleeping room**. **Rudy and girlfriend performed most of the building work**. Acorn was involved but not necessarily capable of technical construction himself. Acorn's parents **do not know H2 exists**. Entrance form and furnishings remain unapproved.

**Both hideouts coexist.** The user's wording “he has multiple” establishes **at least these two**; it does not independently authorize or forbid additional future hideouts. The two are not aliases for the same space.

2. At least **one bookshelf-shaped concealed entrance** is on the hallway wall **across from gaming studio**; which wall is “north/south,” absolute room order, entrance clearances, and the internal H1 geometry remain undecided.
3. **H2 lies within/across a concealed subsection of sleeping room** (precise construction topology open). It has no approved bookshelf door and no approved connection to the hallway beyond ordinary sleeping-room access.

5. The body of a six-month, extremely chubby, quadrupedal brown bear with giant paws must be able to move through eventually selected entrances. This is a future architectural check, not an approved numeric doorway size.

## Privacy and emergency tension — **open**, not silently “solved”

Q66 already assures Mama/ Mommy's care and emergency access to Acorn's personal spaces and Q66 E ensures the hidden retreat does not become a mechanism that automatically prevents their intervention when needed for safety. **Q68 now adds that they don't know of H2.**

These can coexist as world facts **without automatically guaranteeing instantaneous physical rescue in every hypothetical event**. Future room designs should examine:
- Does a trusted nonparent (e.g. Rudy, girlfriend) help in an emergency, and how without violating parental ignorance? Not chosen.
- Does the sleeping-room design leave some non-disclosing emergency means for caregivers to locate/approach Acorn? Not chosen.
- Does the secrecy create real risk and uncertainty, acknowledged as a fictional consequence rather than silently retconning discovery? Not chosen.
- Exact access controls, disclosure thresholds, locks and safety devices remain OPEN.
