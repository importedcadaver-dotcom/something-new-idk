## Individual established main characters — seven roles

### 1. Acorn “Muffin” HoneyDweller — FINAL first name and nickname
- **Species/age:** male brown bear cub, **6 actual months**, roughly human-two-year developmental equivalent under approved early 4:1 development concept; still a genuine baby cub despite unusually early reading and gaming vocabulary.
- **Appearance:** extremely chubby/soft, thick fluffy dark-brown coat/back, nearly all-white belly, cinnamon guard-hair/cheek/muzzle/tail accents, hazel eyes, huge slightly drooping bear ears, unusually enormous paws, extra-wide bear tail, **one deep reddish-cinnamon anatomical RIGHT ear**, and **one lighter sock on anatomical FRONT LEFT forepaw**; mostly quadrupedal. Full reference is visual sheet v0.3.
- **Personality:** all Q45 A–H together depending on situation: chaotic funny toddler, sweet and clingy, highly competitive, emotionally fragile, entitled because of extreme wealth, curious and talkative, shy with strangers but confident with family/audience, deeply motivated by family closeness.
- **Off-camera emotional range (Q62):** stubborn protests, embarrassment/hiding/memory, family reassurance, overload effects, occasional guilt with difficulty apologizing, comfort toys/routines, relationship-dependent comfort.
- **Q63 ranking:** **F+G > E > A > B > C > D**: context and initial resistance most salient; then objects/solitude; then Mama > Mommy > Rudy > Millie as general personal comfort tendency, not a hard rule.
- **Preferences:** likes toys, playing, climbing, running/tag, swimming, gaming, attention; dark red favorite color. Fears snakes/centipedes, dislikes harsh sun, wrong bath temperature, toothbrushing, being scolded/embarrassed and Grandma. Can climb up and struggle to get down.
- **Voice:** tiny extremely high-pitched baby voice normally, with other context-dependent soft, loud, expressive or bear-like sounds. Does not speak/reason as an adult.
- **Channel:** extremely wealthy child celebrity with ownership/ordinary control of his own stream; genuine Fortnite player, game skill imperfect and growing. Later notorious donated-food protest incident exists in prior canon; he understood what he did and later felt embarrassed. No first-stream history or named viewers yet.

### 2. Millie HoneyDweller — FINAL first name
- Female six-month-old brown bear; **Acorn's twin and the only younger-litter sibling**. Her paws are **smaller than Acorn's**; the rest of her physical palette/features have not been approved.
- Doesn't like gaming. Deliberately annoys Acorn, steals possessions, sometimes unplugs his computer, calls him names and laughs at his distress. Jealous of his fame and mothers' attention.
- Can be sweet/polite toward relatives/visitors/Grandma, who favors and mentors her; sometimes protects her brother from outsiders. She is **not** a uniformly evil adult mastermind; her independent interests remain to be defined.
- Q63 D: Acorn **sometimes** looks to Millie for companionship/comfort, but she is the lowest-ranked named family member in his user-specified **general** comfort hierarchy. This does not negate her bullying or occasional defense.

### 3. Rudy HoneyDweller — FINAL first name
- Older male brown bear, first/older-litter child; **teenager-equivalent, in high school**. Exact bear chronological age and grade OPEN.
- Rebellious and delinquent; uses bear-world cigarettes, cannabis and alcohol; Acorn admires and increasingly imitates him.
- He laughed at and praised Acorn's notorious livestream food-protest incident. Both mothers regret following Cynthia's parenting guidance with him and want to repair their relationship; the exact causal blame for delinquency is NOT established.
- Cynthia dislikes him but genuinely likes his polar-bear girlfriend. Q63 C: he can be a source of Acorn's attention/protection/approval, below Mama and Mommy in general comfort priority.

### 4. Rosalind “Rosa” HoneyDweller — Mama, WORKING personal name
- Brown bear; one of Acorn, Millie and Rudy's two loving mothers. **Role “Mama” is approved**; personal names Rosalind/Rosa are **working/provisional**.
- Home-focused, independent and ambitious; a non-gamer trying to understand Acorn's gaming/streaming interests, without being passive or subordinate.
- Especially protective of Acorn around Cynthia; overindulges him sometimes, alongside Mommy. Together the mothers confront interference, regret the relationship deterioration with Rudy, and suspect portions of Cynthia's hidden activities without proof.
- Q63 A: first *named family comfort preference* under broadly comparable circumstances, after contextual factors, resistance and comfort objects. Not automatically his only caregiver or able to comfort instantly.

### 5. Audrey HoneyDweller — Mommy, WORKING personal name
- Brown bear; Cynthia's daughter and Rosa's partner, **Acorn calls her Mommy**. First name Audrey is **working/provisional**.
- Ambitious gamer/businesswoman; CEO and controlling shareholder of the legitimate HoneyDweller corporation after Cynthia retired from day-to-day legitimate management. Exceptional Fortnite player who **never deliberately throws matches for Acorn**.
- Equally affectionate and involved as Rosa; sometimes competes to spoil Acorn; both mothers want to reconcile with Rudy and confront Grandma's interference. They are starting to suspect criminal wrongdoing but lack established proof.
- Q63 B: Acorn seeks reassurance/help from her, particularly where games/streaming are relevant; generally below Mama in the Q63 comfort hierarchy, **not** in parental importance or affection.

### 6. Grandma Cynthia HoneyDweller — WORKING first name
- Brown bear; **Audrey's mother** and sole surviving grandparent, powerful and feared family matriarch, former daily leader of legitimate family enterprise.
- Genuinely dislikes Acorn and Rudy; openly favors **Millie**, whom she deliberately mentors to resemble her younger mischievous self; also genuinely likes Rudy's polar-bear girlfriend.
- Secretly leads an independent clandestine criminal organization, significant international smuggling, finance, intimidation/violence and drug-trade facilitation/power-broker roles. She was responsible for arranging the killings of other grandparents after they tried seizing control; most other main characters' knowledge of this is **unknown**.
- Acorn is quiet, vigilant and afraid around her. **She was not a Q63 comfort option** and cannot be silently promoted into his preferred comfort network. Her name remains provisional.
- **Important:** Both mothers have suspicions, not confirmed knowledge of particular crimes/murders; Millie's knowledge of Grandma's ultimate plans OPEN.

### 7. Rudy's unnamed polar-bear girlfriend — identity established; name OPEN
- Teenage female polar bear; kind, sweet and polite **as well as** rebellious/delinquent, unusually intelligent and calculating. Rudy's girlfriend, liked by Cynthia.
- Family: parents with roughly **30 offspring across numerous litters** (whether she is included in the rounded number unresolved); many remain living together. Parents have reasonably paid jobs, but household finances are strained by family size. Her mother has unusually high fantasy fertility.
- Genuinely befriends some **humans in person**, with contacts through human employees and delinquent networks. Humans do not gain lawful bear-internet accounts; Cynthia's knowledge of these friendships undecided.
- First name, precise age, relationship history, surname, future family-group living choice and many personal details remain OPEN.
