# Q71 — Quiet Under-Bed Basement Access: Character and Safety Consistency v0.1

**Status:** Written-only design interpretation of [direct Q71 B+C+E+F approval](../decisions/Q71_QUIET_MECHANICAL_LIFT_STAIRS_AND_RUDY_ASSIST_B_C_E_F_APPROVED.md). Nothing here is a mechanically functional design or a guarantee of legal/building or biological safety.

## Directly approved access functions

| Access feature | Approved property | Not yet defined |
|---|---|---|
| **Wide, shallow cub-scaled stairs (Q71 B)** | Broad, easy-to-negotiate *concept* for a very young quadrupedal bear cub; not an ordinary human ladder. | Physical angle/rise/tread, position, lighting, grip, usable headroom and real safety. |
| **Quiet mechanical lift (Q71 C + custom)** | A concealed or otherwise hidden **mechanical moving platform** connects levels; its normal movement should be **quiet**. | How the lift itself is concealed; motor/control hardware, mechanism, measurements, power, quietness standard, audible transmission. |
| **Rudy's occasional assistance (Q71 E)** | Rudy **sometimes helps** Acorn enter or exit if Acorn struggles. | Frequency, how help is offered or refused, exact body movements and emergency responsibilities. |
| **Combined functions (Q71 F)** | All selected B/C/E features coexist. | They are not automatically two different doors or independent emergency paths. |

**Not approved:** Q71 **A** gentle ramp, or Q71 **D** as an additional independent design choice of redundant/multiple routes. B+C inherently describe stairs and lift, without fixing a second physical entrance, full failover or escape network.

## Continuity / acceptance tests

1. **Quiet, not unrealistically silent:** No assumption that bear ears or building vibration never detect the lift. User did not specify a numeric acoustics target or machinery design.
2. **Cub physicality:** Acorn's body is mostly quadrupedal with extra-large paws, chubby belly, short legs and young developmental capacities. An adult human ladder, narrow upright standing controls or a requirement to carry him every time would contradict intent.
3. **Agency:** His willingness to enter/leave and Q63 occasional refusal of help remain relevant; Rudy's presence is helpful sometimes, not mandatory.
4. **Privacy:** Rudy does not gain permanent unrestricted permission for the rest of the suite by helping. Mama/Mommy remain unaware of H2 despite their caregiver duty.
5. **Emergency uncertainty:** No guaranteed rescue, structural stability or safe lift-failure scenario has been demonstrated yet; future design must confront them.
6. **Entertainment not streaming:** H2's TV/games (Q70 D) do not turn H2 into a new streaming studio or change Q58–61 display/TTS camera rules.
7. **No invented timeline:** The choice of quiet machinery does not create construction dates, family detection stories, site logistics, incidents or dialogue.
8. **Physical signature:** Preserve Acorn's anatomical **RIGHT deep reddish-cinnamon ear** and **FRONT LEFT lighter-fur paw sock**. No anthropomorphic hands.
9. **Provenance:** Q6–17 original answers remain incompletely recovered; working family first names remain provisional. The other Q44–70 approvals all survive unchanged.
10. **Phase:** Documentation only; no illustrations, audio samples, gameplay, scenes or source code.
