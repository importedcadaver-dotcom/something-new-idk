## Potential consent and safety paper tests — not code or incidents

- **Ordinary sibling visit:** Playroom may welcome interaction, but both **Millie and Rudy need Acorn's permission** before entering/touching possessions.
- **Studio guest:** Acorn may approve or decline ordinarily; a relative's presence does not automatically give them camera control or moderator authority.
- **Sleeping/privacy:** No uninvited everyday sibling walk-ins as a default. Mama/ Mommy may enter when care or emergency access is necessary; precise etiquette is undecided.
- **Hidden nook:** Retreat must not be turned into a 24/7 recording zone or made inaccessible to caregivers during danger. Exact camera policy and lock mechanisms remain OPEN.
- **Acorn is overwhelmed:** Q62 D and Q63 G make refusal/solitude plausible; access and safety rules should not force him to surrender privacy purely because he is emotionally difficult.
- **Possessions:** Q64 G possessiveness and Millie's already established mischievous tendency do not prove she ever entered illegally or stole a particular toy. Such specific incidents require later authorization.
- **Mobility:** Connection, gates and accessibility must fit a mostly quadrupedal six-month-old fat/fluffy cub with unusually large paws; no ordinary human hands.
