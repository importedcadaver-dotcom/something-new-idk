## Unresolved consistency questions — track; do not 'fix' by invention

- **Cub access:** A six-month chubby quadrupedal brown bear has large paws and limited climbing/downward mobility; **how he reaches a below-floor level safely remains undecided**.

- **Historical canon timing:** The basement exists in approved backstory, but when it was made, what triggered its construction and whether it was ever discovered remain unapproved.
- **Nook vs basement size:** Q69 approves excavation of a 'basement', not numerical size or guaranteed lavish furnishings; do not upgrade it into a second apartment.
