## Unresolved design-risk register

- **Physical:** hidden safe access under Acorn's bed through floorboards; safe cub movement between levels; exit during emergency; structurally sound excavation and floor loads.
- **Habitability:** air, moisture, power, lighting, sanitation, heating, egress and medical access; none automatically certified by 'luxury'.
- **Discovery:** how extensive earthwork, furnishing and electronics reached the space without alerting Mama, Mommy or estate staff; no cover story or hidden helper yet selected.
- **Social:** what items Rudy brings and whether his girlfriend and Acorn consent to store them; no choice on drugs, weapons, contraband, adult content or real criminal behavior in the basement.
- **Privacy:** H1 parent-known versus H2 parent-unknown, Millie's knowledge open, and Q66 parental safety obligations unresolved.
- **Time:** whether it was built before Acorn's present fame; what 'gradually evolving' means in chronology; no timeline set.
