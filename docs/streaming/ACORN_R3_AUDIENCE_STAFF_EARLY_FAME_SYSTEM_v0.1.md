**Strong design constraints:** Never permit a paying donor to force Acorn to eat, to override his ban list or to keep TTS playing after he pauses. **Conflicts that need ratification rather than automation:** when staff can block a threat over Acorn's objection; formal moderator ban limits; filtering family priority; which senders are allowed to contact him privately; how children may receive money; how both moms are notified of dangerous content under **G1**.
