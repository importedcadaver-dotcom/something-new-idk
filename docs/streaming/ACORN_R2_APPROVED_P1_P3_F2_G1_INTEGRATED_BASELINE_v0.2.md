## 1. Approved input composition, no simulated gameplay shortcuts

- **P1 + P3 combined**: **two large forepaw directional controls** serve real continuous gameplay movement/look, accompanied by **advanced customizable adaptive switches/sticks/action inputs**. They work together as one planned interface, not two separate alternative game systems. 
- Forepaw control must be **actually Acorn-generated**, not staff moving or aiming secretly or AI playing for him. He is legitimately imperfect and improving at real **Fortnite**.
- **Physical plausibility still OPEN**: exact motion axis allocation, accessible auxiliary action positions, cub support, control surfaces, accidental hits, reach/force/latency, and how he actuates a button while each forepaw is directing an analog input. His paws are not human hands and he cannot be presumed to sit unsupported upright like an adult at a computer.
- **Not approved:** P2 head tracking as chosen input, a fixed button count, a specific Xbox/Sony brand, voice-only aim, auto-win mechanics, streaming desktop layout or a hand-pinch interface.

## 2. Approved platform model F2 — familiar streamer experience, DIFFERENT BEAR SERVICE

- **F2** chooses a **fictionalized Twitch-like streaming service adapted to bear society and its laws**. The service resembles a familiar live-gaming ecosystem, but **it is not literal present-day Twitch**.
- **Exact name/handle, publisher/licensing, TOS, age thresholds and legal guardian responsibility are UNCHOSEN.** Keep its bear-only internet, class/species social structures and moderators coherent with already-approved fictional civilization.
- **Fortnite is real/recognizable game canon** for Acorn; how in-world bear users access the game through bear-only networking remains OPEN. A bear-native livestream service is not the same as an Epic game account.
- Do not quietly replace F2 with generic platform F1 because earlier assistant preferred F1, or with real-world Twitch F3 because of the Twitch-like aesthetic.
- Real Twitch's age rules and Epic Cabined Account documentation are **real-world context**, not automatically the fictional bear platform's policy. No literal account is created.

## 3. Approved G1 + sometimes unreasonable Mommy — two levels of reality

### G1: The rights and limits

- **Ordinary channel:** Acorn chooses go-live/offline, Fortnite/game/media, stream guests (including whether Mommy participates on air), bans/unbans, gifts and food refusals, and approved TTS pause/skip/resume. Technical bear staff execute authorized ordinary commands; they do not secretly own his creative or player decisions.
- **Routine care:** Mama/ Mommy generally **negotiate** with Acorn about nonurgent care; their caregiving obligations remain real and he cannot be assumed to ignore all essential care forever.
- **Serious/immediate danger:** Mama or Mommy may intervene **temporarily and proportionately** for his protection. The exact hazards/triggers, physical devices, staff powers and legal procedures are **OPEN**; a theoretical temporary protective intervention is NOT a platform-wide auto-kill switch, unrestricted permanent admin takeover or general parental displeasure power.
- Q66 caregiver access and Q68 H2 parental ignorance both remain; no knowledge teleportation.

### Mommy: user-added character nuance, not unearned administrative powers

**Mommy is sometimes unreasonably strict**. This is *directly approved*. She can be **genuinely excessive or inflexible on occasion**, rather than merely “firm but fair.” But she is not **always** strict, nor always right, nor her family's default villain. She still loves/indulges Acorn, plays Fortnite extremely well, will not intentionally lose, leads the legitimate family corporation and is a distinct involved mother alongside Mama.

**Important distinction:** Being **unreasonably strict in temperament and parenting behavior** can generate friction with Acorn and Mama, including wanting rules Acorn opposes. It does **not by itself** grant her permanent stream ban/list authority, independent technical capture controls, parent-owned channel title, or a routine-care override specifically excluded from G1. The exact rule she imposes and the real-world-family consequence of a refusal are not established.

**G1 and Mommy strictness are simultaneously canon**, not competing options; characters can sometimes push beyond what others consider reasonable without authors predeciding who wins.

## 6. Five targeted acceptance tests for later work

**R2A-01:** A proposed mapping for **P1+P3** supports two analog controls and at least one independent action with Acorn's own paws; if it can't, mark feasibility OPEN and don't invent AI gameplay.

**R2A-02:** A platform is described as familiar/Twitch-like, **fictional bear-native rules**, without current literal Twitch underage account contradictions or a newly invented platform name.

**R2A-03:** Routine-care conflict between Mommy and Acorn is plausible because she is **sometimes unreasonably strict**, without declaring an automatic parent takeover of his entire channel.

**R2A-04:** Serious actual danger can lead to temporary, proportionate care action; not a new permanent editorial policy. Acorn's normal ability to ban an audience member, refuse food or ask Mommy to leave a stream remains.

**R2A-05:** The story acknowledges that a strict mother may disagree with another loving mother or Acorn, while both mothers retain their distinct character and are not granted knowledge of H2.

**All five are paper design consistency conditions**, NOT physical/safety testing, runtime implementations or invented historical scenes.
