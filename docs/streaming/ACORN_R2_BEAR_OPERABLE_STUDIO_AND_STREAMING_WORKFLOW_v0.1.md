## 1. Non-negotiable canon and physical feasibility

**[APPROVED]** Acorn “Muffin” HoneyDweller is a six-actual-month male **brown bear cub** with broadly naturalistic early body development, extreme chubbiness/fluffiness, very large paws, short legs, mostly quadrupedal posture, hazel eyes, almost-white belly, deep reddish-cinnamon **anatomical RIGHT ear** and lighter **anatomical FRONT LEFT forepaw sock**. His infant voice is normally very high pitched, and reading is exceptionally advanced while judgment remains toddler-like. He **genuinely** plays **Fortnite**, currently not automatically expertly, and can learn through practice. Do NOT replace Fortnite with a generic toddler puzzle, give him human fingers, or assign a game-winning AI to play for him.

**[APPROVED]** He has a private dedicated **gaming/streaming studio**, sleeping room and playroom connected by a short hallway. Ordinary studio guests require Acorn's permission; his family and tech staff may have separately specified care/safety responsibilities. **[APPROVED]** Acorn controls ordinary channel decisions: go/live/offline, content, invitations, requests, refuses gifts/food/challenges, bans/unbans, even whether his gaming Mommy joins. ## 2. Physical ergonomic and input architecture — **OPTIONS**, not selected equipment

### Physical constraints that any option must satisfy

| Challenge | Why it matters for Acorn | Paper acceptance gate |
|---|---|---|
| **Front paw anatomy** | No opposable human thumbs/fingers assumed; paw pads and claws are not a keyboard grip | Large-force-tolerant, broadly targetable surfaces; no required precision finger pinch |
| **Two action axes** | A real fast-paced game needs concurrent movement, camera/aim and discrete actions | Explain how movement and look can operate **at the same time**, not merely one huge on/off button |
| **Chubby low center of mass** | A six-month-old fat cub cannot comfortably stand on two hind legs at a human desktop for extended sessions | Stable low rest-support or ergonomic platform, front-paw access and no long unsupported bipedal posture |
| **Cognitive/attention limits** | Reads remarkably well yet is impulsive and can be overstimulated | Few clearly distinguished input zones, learnable mapping, recoverability and context cues without adult reading burdens |
| **Physical stamina** | Reaching all controls, moving head and holding postures can fatigue him | Movement range, resting, optional adjustments; no fixed session endurance claims |
| **Sensory needs** | Loud alerts/noise and bright sun can overwhelm; baby voice quiet/fast | Sensitive sound and light levels; don't force constant TTS or flashing overlays |
| **Independent agency** | User demands a true streamer/gamer rather than staff pretending he's playing | Acorn generates real game movement/look/action commands, with his skill and mistakes visibly causal |
| **Species/age uniqueness** | Adult bear controller dimensions won't necessarily fit an infant | Cub-specific adjustable geometry; exact measurements remain open until individually decided |

### Four candidate interface families (PROPOSED, noncanonical)

**P1 — Twin forepaw analog pads + surrounding large buttons (RECOMMENDED PAPER BASELINE).** A low cub-reachable gaming surface has two directional input zones usable by his **front paws** without relying on thumb-grips. Broad side/nearby pad buttons cover jump, interact, item, aim or other remapped functions. A reclining/lying/chest-supported posture may reduce the burden of forepaws being lifted. **Feasibility remains experimental**: if both front paws must control analog movement/look continuously, button access, weight support and combat tempo are nontrivial. Do not invent 100% capable controls.

**P2 — One analog paw control + head/muzzle pointing for camera.** A forepaw provides movement while deliberate head movement sets look direction. A second forepaw/body panel triggers game actions. **Trade-off:** aiming with head and reacting physically could be tiring, imprecise and camera-visible; do not assume superior precision or a real-world tested bear tracking device.

**P3 — Adaptive switch array plus one accessible joystick/pad and a separate head/voice auxiliary input.** Modular pad/switch input can map actions; analog movement/look still needs solving. **Real accessibility devices demonstrate switch/joystick modularity, NOT automatic compatibility with a bear cub**.

**P4 — Hybrid Acorn play + non-game technical staff assistance.** A production worker may switch scene sources, adjust audio/chat overlays, troubleshoot a signal or execute Acorn's commands while **not aiming/moving/shooting on Acorn's behalf**. This is compatible with P1–P3 (an assistance *role*, not a substitute for two-axis game input). **Exact worker commands and moderation rights remain OPEN.**

**R2 recommendation:** Work from P1 + P4 as a draft **for further owner approval**: lowest risk of inventing mind-reading, AI game competence or impossible hand dexterity. Keep P2/P3 as alternatives rather than quietly declaring P1 selected.

### Map game actions as functional classes, not fictionalized gameplay

| Input class | Genuine player responsibility | Possible paw interface (PROPOSED) | Verification challenge |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Travel/strafe** | Directly and continuously move in world | Left broad multidirectional control | Can he move while looking? |
| **Camera/look/aim** | Directly change view/aim | Right broad multidirectional control or head tracker | Can he change camera without accidental paw taps? |
| **Jump/crouch/sprint/interact** | Trigger discrete actions | Large tactile buttons or pads in reachable area | Can he reach while maintaining analog input? |
| **Weapon/select/build/menu** | Choose items or game actions | Mode-aware remappable pads | Does any required double-press exceed dexterity? No one else may secretly choose to win |
| **Start/pause/end stream** | Ordinary broadcast command, NOT game action | Separate obvious broadcast-control surface | Can't accidentally stream while trying to jump |
| **Chat/TTS pause/skip/resume** | User-approved Q61 D control | Distinct simple paw-control(s) with feedback | Can he interrupt TTS immediately without staff acting as a hidden veto? |
| **Emergency ask-for-help** | Acorn may request attention or an adult may notice danger | Independently reachable call mechanism **PROPOSED** | Does not silently create 24/7 surveillance or formal override rule |

**Real-life analogs:** Microsoft Xbox Adaptive Controller supports external switches, buttons, joysticks via 3.5mm/USB; Sony Access controller supports remapped controls, stick settings/profiles. Epic Fortnite supports a **Custom** controller mapping. These are *design precedents*, not canon brand placements, bear safety proofs, or guaranteed cross-platform support. See [R2 external research](../research/R2_ACCESSIBLE_GAMING_REAL_PLATFORM_RULES_RESEARCH_2026-11-08.md).

### Workflow traces — paper examples, not past events

**Normal spontaneous go-live:** Acorn wants Fortnite, uses bear-first inputs; staff optionally help configure the broadcast pipeline **at his request**; his selected overlay displays game and camera. A parent does NOT automatically decide the guest list or content. Some accounts/security control may be constrained by future bear law [OPEN].

**During high chat volume:** Staff may filter spam under Q61 E; only selected messages reach TTS. Acorn can pause, skip, resume; a moderator can advise. Whether their moderation bypasses Acorn's own ban decisions or who can stop harassing strangers is owner-policy open.

**No food obligation:** An audience request or donation does not force physical food delivery/consumption or Acorn to keep a stream running. HoneyDweller kitchen staff and channel staff are different functions. Existing famous food incident is old history only, not a new episode.

**Closing stream:** Acorn ordinarily says to end/offline, and staff may help execute. A temporary technical failure may interrupt the signal but does not change the canon that Acorn owns/controls ordinary channel policy. Emergency actions require ratification in [R2 governance design packet](R2_CHANNEL_CAREGIVING_MODERATION_GOVERNANCE_OPTIONS_v0.1.md).

## 6. Bear-only platform, real Fortnite and actual external constraints

### Current public reference facts (not fictional canon)

- **Twitch Terms of Service modified 2026-08-12**: Twitch service minimum age **13** (or higher jurisdictional minimum); minors above minimum need parental supervision. A literal current-world Twitch account **for a six-month-old cannot simply be treated as compliant**. [Official Twitch ToS](https://www.twitch.tv/p/terms-of-service).
- **Epic Cabined Accounts**: Under-13/under-digital-consent users may play Fortnite while certain features (voice/free-text chat, purchases, external service sign-in) remain restricted pending guardian approval. This **does not grant a six-month-old ownership of an unrelated livestream service**. [Epic official FAQ](https://www.epicgames.com/site/cabinedaccounts?lang=en-US).
- **Fortnite Custom controller mappings** are publicly documented, but mapping options do not establish independent infant-bear dexterity. [Epic controller mapping](https://www.epicgames.com/help/c-34254770/c-33726977/kyfyh-tkwyn-whdh-thkm-fy-fortnite-a23146860).

### Candidate BEAR-WORLD platform models (all PROPOSED)

| Platform model | Fiction-world design consequence | Consistency risk |
|---|---|---|
| **F1 — Fictional bear-native service** (**recommendation**, NOT selected) | Bears use own bear-only network, bear-legal childhood/guardian safeguards, streaming moderation, channel and ownership rules. Real games could be licensed/ported through explicit separate world rules | Need to define how genuine Fortnite servers/game rights reach bear-only world |
| **F2 — Bear-world fictionalized platform with recognizable “Twitch-like” convention** | Familiar interface feel and culture, but rules, institutions and brand possibly distinct; any literal external brand usage and rights unresolved | Cannot silently borrow current Twitch terms unchanged |
| **F3 — Literal unchanged real-world Twitch/Epic services** | Would require explicit legal/age/account and bear-network exceptions; **current Twitch age rule conflicts with literal ordinary account premise** | Not recommended unless user wants to rewrite world rules explicitly |

**Recommended R2 judgment:** F1 + explicitly described *real Fortnite availability via lawful fictional bear-world distribution* is the most self-consistent **proposal**. The exact in-world provider and service name remain OPEN and not automatically approved by recommending F1.

A parent could have legal obligations in a fictional universe without being a hidden vetoing “true creative owner,” **but this specific legal interpretation still needs world-rule approval**.
