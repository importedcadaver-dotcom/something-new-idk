## 1. Already approved rights and constraints

1. **Acorn owns and controls ordinary channel decisions**: go live/offline, what real games/media to stream, whether to invite/evict an ordinary guest (including gamer Mommy), ordinary bans/unbans, optional viewer games/challenges, rejecting food and donations. He is young, volatile and not an expert but he is **not a puppet of production staff or parents**.
2. **Caregivers remain real caregivers**: Q66 Mama and Mommy may enter for genuine care/emergencies; their ordinary presence should respect his privacy and needs. This is **not** approval of remote spying, a constant veto or a specific emergency shutdown control.
3. **Staff assist:** Bear moderators can filter spam/inappropriate content; TTS prioritization capabilities exist and Acorn can **pause, skip and resume** spoken messages. Exact moderation rules and whether staff can issue independent bans/kill the stream are unresolved.
4. **Privacy:** Q65 sleeping/play/studio rooms and Q66 sibling consent intact; physical webcam location and permitted shooting angles OPEN. **H2's existence is still unknown to Mama and Mommy**; an emergency model cannot conjure knowledge of H2.
5. **Age and emotion:** Acorn's early reading prowess does not make him mature enough to consent to everything; nor does a tantrum automatically establish a life-threatening emergency.
6. **Legal/financial layer OPEN:** He is wealthy and personally controls ordinary channel policy. How a fictional bear society legally handles a young cub's account, money, contracts, guardian duties and safety is not decided.
7. **External services' rules are not bear-world rules by default:** Current Twitch is age-13-minimum; real Fortnite has young-player account features. This points toward **fictional platform/age law decisions** but selects none; [R2 research](../research/R2_ACCESSIBLE_GAMING_REAL_PLATFORM_RULES_RESEARCH_2026-11-08.md).

## 2. Three separable authority layers

| Layer | Canon authority | **Proposed** allocation | Still OPEN |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Editorial / ordinary show choice** | Acorn is final ordinary decision-maker | Acorn requests content, controls whom to invite, ordinary bans and gift refusals | In-world legal limits, exceptions in abuse/medical danger |
| **Technical / staff execution** | Bear staff and moderators exist, spam filters possible | They keep the capture/input/TTS running, warn of faults, implement Acorn's ordinary commands | Accounts/credentials, staff preemptive safety action, process for malicious chat |
| **Child-care and genuine danger** | Mama/Mommy have legitimate care/emergency access | Proportionate, temporary interventions at clear danger only; routine care via dialogue/choices | Triggers, decision makers, practical ability to pause/end broadcast, reviews/consent |

**Most important anti-retcon:** Even if an adult legally handles liability, the premise cannot be quietly rewritten to say the adult is the true ordinary channel owner who decides Acorn's game, ban list and on-screen guests.

## 3. Three policy packages for a future owner decision (NONE approved)

**G1 — Acorn-first daily choices + proportionate emergency intervention (recommended).** Ordinary streams: Acorn decides; routine care: parent negotiates/asks or provides options around finishing a match, while retaining genuine care responsibility; urgent serious harm: caregiver may temporarily intervene enough to protect him, followed by return of ordinary channel decisions. Tech team executes only permitted/necessary instructions, logs material interruptions. **OPEN** definitions of serious risk, max intrusion, visibility and authorization; not a licensed control mechanism.

**G2 — Cooperative family-care window.** Same ordinary autonomy; parents and Acorn jointly agree in advance on a flexible care/rest window (not a rigid stream schedule); urgent danger intervention proposed as above. **Risk:** User has not approved routine schedules or precommitments. Avoid treating a rejected request as automatic permission to seize the stream.

**G3 — Broader parent authority for ordinary care.** Parents can temporarily pause certain stream activities for necessary routine care even if Acorn resists, while preserving his ability to pick content, bans, guest invitations and ordinary channel ownership. **Risk:** This is a substantially stronger override than the current canon. **Cannot be adopted without explicit owner choice.**

**Do not offer “absolutely no adult intervention in urgent danger” as a desirable default**, since it conflicts with Q66's approved caregiver emergency access. **Do not assume an automatic moderation/parent kill switch exists** under any package until the owner selects an implementation.

## 4. Suggested severity distinction — conceptual only

| Circumstance | Approved baseline | Proposed G1 response pattern | Not established |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acorn dislikes losing a Fortnite match | Can get upset; may demand retry; owns ordinary show | Mommy coaches fairly/doesn't intentionally lose; Acorn can pause/change content | Parents shut down channel simply because he is annoyed |
| A parent asks for brushing, bath or rest | Ordinary care responsibility; Acorn sometimes resists | Negotiate harmless timing or approach; do not redefine tantrum as medical crisis | Exact nondeferrable routine task and consequences |
| Acorn gets overstimulated by TTS | Q61 pause/skip/resume; Q62 overwhelm | Acorn can interrupt TTS; moderators apply available spam filtering | Parent automatically takes ban control |
| Spam or grossly inappropriate viewer messages | Q61 moderator filtering exists | Moderators filter before TTS with explainable limits; Acorn decides ordinary bans | Exact content filters, moderator appeals, invisible parent ban list |
| Threatening real-time external event / medical emergency | Care/emergency rights exist | Adults act proportionately to protect Acorn, possibly pausing exposure | Who presses what control or when; platform notification policy |
| Gift/food pressure | Acorn can refuse; no forced consumption | Staff refuse/stop handling gift when he directs, without coercion | Mandatory physical delivery, staff force-feeding |
| Staff encoder signal fault | Technical assistants exist | Staff explain and fix; cannot invent game decisions on Acorn's behalf | Credentials, outage policy, automatic restream/rebroadcast |
| Acorn secretly in H2; no one knows where he is | H2 exists, unknown to both mothers | Emergency search might begin from observable cues, not magical knowledge | Rescue guarantees, floor hatch access for adults, underground safety |

**The above is NOT an encoded event response list.** Real character autonomy still means parents, Rudy, Millie and staff may have competing intentions and can decline ordinary requests, without having a prewritten incident.
