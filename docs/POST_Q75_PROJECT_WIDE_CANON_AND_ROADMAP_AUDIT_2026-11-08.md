## 5. Important counterfactual test — “Mama walks into Acorn's bedroom”

**This is a PAPER TEST, NOT a new enacted scene or canonical past incident.**

**Given:** Mama can enter for genuine care under Q66; Acorn retains ordinary privacy and can decline requests; his known H1 differs from hidden H2; Acorn/Rudy/girlfriend know H2 exists; Mama and Mommy don't.

- If Mama enters the bedroom, she **does not automatically discover H2**. She may see the bed, Acorn, a toy, or the ordinary room; what she observes depends on an unchosen position and action.
- If Acorn is moving into the **cramped outer gap under bed**, Mama *could* notice that movement. This does **not prove** she sees a hatch or knows what is beyond the bed.
- If Acorn is anxious and conceals his activity, that can follow Q62 embarrassment/stubbornness and Q45 independence. But **whether he lies, says he is seeking a toy, asks for privacy, cries, retreats, or tells Mama the truth is CONTEXTUAL and NOT decided**. A sample joke from chat is not a new canon event, dialogue quote or obligatory scripted response.
- Mama's ordinary caring response does not make her a covert criminal investigator or omniscient; nor can her safety rights be used to *declare she always finds the basement*.
- If an actual emergency occurs while H2 is unknown to her, what she can physically access or notice remains an **unresolved safety and causality risk**, not guaranteed success.

**Acceptance criterion:** Later hypothetical incidents must obey spatial visibility, each character's knowledge, age-limited communication, attachment, and consequential autonomy, without granting clairvoyance or scripted discoveries.
