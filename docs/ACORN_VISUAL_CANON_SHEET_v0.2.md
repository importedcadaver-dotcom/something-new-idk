## Character identity and silhouette

Acorn (“Muffin”) HoneyDweller is a **six-month-old male brown bear cub**. He must retain naturalistic bear morphology, a predominantly **quadrupedal posture**, bear muzzle, ears, paws and claws. He is exceptionally **chubby/fat and visibly well fed**, with a large rounded soft baby face, full cheeks, dense thick fluffy coat, short stout legs, an **extra-wide tail**, **very large/relatively long ears with a slight droop**, and **abnormally enormous paws** (larger than his same-age twin Millie's). His physical traits are stylized in *proportion* by owner approval but must not be converted into humanoid anatomy or a stuffed toy.

No exact weight, body length, paw ratio, tail length or ear droop angle has been chosen.

## Continuity and negative constraints

**Must preserve:** Very plump juvenile brown bear, floppy-ish large bear ears, oversized left-sock paw, extra-wide tail, dark body/back, white belly, cinnamon guard hairs plus subtle facial accents and broad-tail coloring, one red-cinnamon ear with side OPEN, hazel eyes, naturalistic quadruped.

**Must not assume:** Specific ear side, white sock instead of merely lighter, matching paw socks, a matching red ear, beige instead of white belly, cinnamon replacing dark body/back, mandatory added flank/neck patches, extra cinnamon legs, scattered stripes/spots instead of hairs, a long fox/cat tail, rabbit ears, human hands, explicit eye shape, or individual marks other than those chosen.
