## Gating acceptance tests for proposing early stream incidents

1. **Character:** A viewer challenge changes behavior via Acorn's established desires/fears/traits, not random scripted outrage.
2. **Development:** Speech, comprehension, reach and energy are plausible for the setting's unusually able six-month bear cub; no adult assumptions.
3. **Room/tech:** Each broadcast shot and input is physically viable for a plump, primarily quadrupedal cub with large paws.
4. **Agency:** Acorn can genuinely stop TTS, change streams, refuse demands, ban viewers and eject guests within approved channel power.
5. **Audience:** A first chatter is not automatically a permanent friend, villain or troll; named characters/roles need owner approval.
6. **Moderation:** Actual filtering and response authority make sense **before** an incident; don't manufacture drama by unrealistically disabling all protection.
7. **Timeline:** No contradiction with already canon viral protest, embarrassed reaction, platform warning or viewership bands; no invented fixed first date.
8. **World:** Bear-only internet, bear-controlled society, family wealth, moms' positions and Cynthia's secret illicit organization preserved.
9. **Provenance:** Q6–17 original individual message evidence remains incomplete and is not retroactively marked verified.
10. **Phase restriction:** No scenes, actual roleplay or gameplay without explicit later permission.
