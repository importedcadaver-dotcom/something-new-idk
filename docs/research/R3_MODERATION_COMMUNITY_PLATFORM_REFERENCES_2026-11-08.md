## Observations and limits

1. Real services distinguish **channel ownership, trusted moderators, dashboard editing and automated filtering**. Using these categories as a planning vocabulary does **NOT** select the exact permissions in bear world.
2. Both real services provide ways to reduce harmful or high-volume chat exposure. User-approved Q61 already includes moderators filtering inappropriate/spam content and Acorn's **pause/skip/resume** commands. R3 should preserve those without importing all features of the real Twitch/YouTube products.
3. Some F2 users may be famous, wealthy, manipulative or simply child fans; however none of these sources proves the *user's fictional* audience origins, demographics, paywall behavior or fandom metrics. Those require original fictional-world design and owner approval.
4. There is **no evidence** in these real sources that actual fixed viewer concurrency determines a follower count or donations. Never derive numeric totals from Acorn's approved concurrent ranges alone.
5. Child-safety age laws on real Twitch and Epic were reviewed separately in [R2 external research](R2_ACCESSIBLE_GAMING_REAL_PLATFORM_RULES_RESEARCH_2026-11-08.md). F2 is a **fictionalized Twitch-like bear platform, not literal Twitch**. The owner chose F2 directly, superseding older F1 recommendation.
6. A creator's ability to reject gifts and ban ordinary viewers is **Acorn's binding fictional canon**. Real-life service staff role pages are evidence for *possibilities*, not a reason to hand Acorn's channel authority to staff or Mommy.

## Reference-to-canon guardrails

**Approved in world already:** Bear-only internet, real Fortnite played, Acorn's normal channel self-rule, 500–2,000 pre-event and 5,000–20,000 post-event **concurrent**, known food incident causing publicity, platform warning only, richly varied fan categories, extensive bear moderation/production staff, Q61 spam/TTS controls, **F2** fictionalized streamer service, **P1+P3** controls, **G1** care policy, Mommy sometimes unreasonably strict.

**NOT approved just by public research:** AutoMod by brand name in bear world, Shield Mode, named VIP/moderator, exact flagging categories, YouTube style two-tier mod role, unique viewer estimate, specific gift rates, sponsor contract, first clip uploader, account registration/hardware, latest charity drive, past trolling event or a game platform's real live legal rules.

**R3 outcome:** Primary-source feature research gives a reasonable architectural analogy for a scalable F2 audience moderation workflow without accidentally canonizing product details or granting Acorn's staff/game platform powers the user hasn't chosen.
