## Valid inference boundaries

- The user explicitly chose **intelligent bears with naturalistic animal shape and enhanced dexterity**, **six-month-old twins**, **~4 human developmental years per 1 actual bear year in early development**, and **80–120 actual bear-year lifespan**. These fictional rules **override any attempt to import wild-bear lifespan/reproduction norms unchanged**, but **do not automatically grant adults' physical reach or mechanical strength to a cub**.
- The user's fictional polar-bear mother with ~30 cubs across many litters is an intentional departure from wild polar-bear life history. **Quantifying exact litter counts, pregnancy periods or which offspring are living at home requires owner decisions**, not arithmetic guessing.
- A large luxurious H2 with quiet mechanical lift, under-bed access and unseen construction can be **fictionally asserted** and preserved as approved facts. It is not *scientifically demonstrated*, and it is **not yet causally explained** how excavation, spoils, ventilation, noise, delivery logistics, structural stability, authorities and staff oversight were reconciled.
- **Safety and concealment can conflict**: measures that would help a real occupant escape or summon help may make hidden construction easier to detect. That is a worldbuilding trade-off, not a request to engineer concealment techniques.
- Exact code requirements depend on occupancy, location, classification and adopted edition. **No owner-approved location/jurisdiction or construction details exist**, so the codes are risk analogies rather than fictional-law citations.
