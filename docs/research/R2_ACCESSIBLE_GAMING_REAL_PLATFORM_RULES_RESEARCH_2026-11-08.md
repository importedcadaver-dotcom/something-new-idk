## Technical reasoning: real two-axis game skill vs infant-bear body

**Continuous tasks:** Real-time competitive game play requires locomotion plus camera/aim, often concurrent jumping, action and item management. An adapter with a single oversized “press to play” button does not by itself solve simultaneous multidimensional control. To keep Acorn's **genuine game competence and genuine mistakes**, each action needs a causal Acorn-origin input.

**Real accessibility precedent:** Modular switches/joysticks, remapping, deadzones, toggles and accessible mounting show that controllers **need not be standard handheld human gamepads**. These precedents support **investigating a bear-compatible fantasy variant** with broad paw targets, body support and independent movement/look controls.

**Unproved claims to avoid:** No real research has tested a bear cub on these controllers; don't claim physiological fit, safety, training success, top-level Fortnite performance, literal trademark compatibility or a precise learning rate. Acorn has unusually advanced reading, not human hands or adult executive function.

**Biology/ergonomics inference (not clinical or engineering proof):** Cub paws and weight-bearing posture complicate continuous manipulation. A low chest-supported or lying workstation, two forepaw-reachable analog surfaces, optional auxiliary head/muzzle controls and a simple separate TTS panel are *proposals* to check, not already selected items. A real test would require body measurements, movement envelope, fatigue evaluation, safety, and independence of two-axis input.

**Age labels:** The fantasy “one real bear year ~ four human developmental years” describes **development**, not automatic equivalence to service-provider chronological age or local law. You cannot rationalize unchanged Twitch ToS merely by multiplying Acorn's age. Nor does Epic's Fortnite allowance automatically validate ownership of a live broadcast platform.

**Moderation distinction:** Epic in-game voice/text restrictions and a bear-native livestream's viewer chat/TTS are **two different communications channels**. Whether links/cross-service chat are allowed must be chosen for the fictional institution. Stream TTS filtering of high viewer loads is canonically present as a capability (Q61) but exact adult moderation powers are unchosen.

## R2 recommendations — NOT user approval

- **R2 hardware draft:** broad-paw analog input and accessible switches rather than a literal human keyboard/gamepad, with body support and distinct TTS controls; verify actual two-axis input rather than add automated aim.
- **R2 world draft:** fictional bear-native platform with explicit separate bear-only game distribution or authentication to maintain real-game identity. Do not lock platform name or law without owner.
- **R2 safety/governance draft:** distinguish child creative control, backstage technical assistance, routine caregiver needs and genuine urgent intervention; legal signatory structure is a separate explicit decision.
- **R2 documentation exit:** No actual hardware model, streaming account, server, parental-control configuration, product purchase, gameplay or voice/video capture is created.

**Source scope:** The linked official pages support the claims directly attributed to them. No third-party invented bear-specific controller is being treated as an existing real product.
