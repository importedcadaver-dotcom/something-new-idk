## 2. Decision hierarchy

**First — mandatory anatomy and identity fidelity gate (highest priority explicitly selected):** An art direction must be able to retain the recognizable same six-month-old chubby naturalistic brown bear, his unusual paws/ears/tail, dark-brown coat/white belly/cinnamon coloring, hazel eyes, front-left sock, and single red-cinnamon **anatomical RIGHT ear** (Q55). If not, it fails regardless of other aesthetics.

**Second — five approved evaluative criteria A/C/D/E/F:** Determine PASS, CONCERN or FAIL for cuteness, recognizability, emotional range, streamer screenshot credibility and multi-image consistency.

**Third — qualitative trade-offs:** Report comparative strengths/weaknesses. The user's "highest priority" **does not** authorize invented numerical weights, an overall numerical score, or a default overall winner.

**Clarification:** Q54's option B (“Anatomical realism”) was **not independently checked**, yet the separate **highest-priority selection** explicitly said “Anatomy and character fidelity.” That makes anatomy a **binding cross-cutting quality requirement**, not a retroactively checked B option. Also Q4/Q48 naturalistic anatomy is previously approved canon.

## 4. Q54 selected criteria — qualitative evaluation rubric

| Q54 | PASS — what evidence would establish it | CONCERN — revise/check | FAIL — reject |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Cutest baby bear** | Fat, fluffy round baby silhouette, huge paws, sweet natural expression with appealing 6-month proportions | Cute close-up but stiff/older-looking full body | Generic skinny animal, plush doll or ugly distorted proportions |
| **C. Recognizable Acorn** | Every iconic approved color and asymmetry is retained at the appropriate viewing scale | Sock/ear/eye hard to distinguish in small frames but reference close-up accurate | Wrong sock side, extra symmetrical ear, missing white belly or unapproved marking |
| **D. Expressive personality** | Distinct mischievous/sweet/shy/upset expressions possible with consistent anatomy and baby voice implied | Some subtle emotions indistinguishable in wide shot | Same frozen face for all emotions, or human facial structure required |
| **E. Believable streaming screenshot** | Game/chat/Acorn/webcam/overlay hierarchy cohesive, plausible bear-operated technology and physical scale | UI distracts, Acorn too small or game legibility compromised | Fake gibberish chat/labels, game unrelated to real games, nonsensical UI |
| **F. Multi-image consistency** | Same appearance and proportions across close-up, full-body, 3/4, side, rear and streaming composition | Slight palette/light variation acceptable but signature details need fixes | Different bear from frame to frame; red ear or sock changes side |

**No numeric weights assigned.** A highly expressive but wrong-bodied result cannot compensate for fidelity gate failure. The illustrative rubric does not itself decide how many corrections are allowed or how to grade future art without viewing actual evidence.
