# Acorn — Eight-View Art Comparison Blueprint v0.1

**Status:** Text-only design blueprint based on **Q56 APPROVED A–H**.  
**Authority:** [Q56 direct decision](../decisions/Q56_ALL_REFERENCE_VIEWS_APPROVED.md) · [Q54 quality gates](ACORN_STYLE_EVALUATION_RUBRIC_Q54_v0.1.md) · [Q53 six candidate directions](ACORN_STYLE_COMPARISON_Q53_v0.1.md).  
**IMPORTANT:** **NO image generation, screenshots, gameplay, story scenes, coding or actual camera capture is authorized**. Every “view” here means a *planned, written reference composition*.

## 0. Comparison protocol

The **same** approved Acorn identity is the subject of all eight future views. If comparative imagery is ever separately authorized, each proposed art direction A–E uses the same anatomical reference and the same or meaningfully equivalent composition; F is evaluated as a streamer overlay / presentation layer paired with those rendering styles. The goal is to avoid comparing an impressive close-up from one style against a tiny distant cub from another.

**Highest-priority mandatory pass/fail gate:** Anatomy and character fidelity, selected by the user for Q54. A candidate that changes his anatomy or approved markings fails before cuteness, expression, UI coherence or multi-image consistency can be compared.

### Locked anatomical identity

- Acorn (“Muffin”) HoneyDweller: male, actual age six months, **brown bear**, primarily quadrupedal, baby-bear anatomy with enhanced bear-specific paw dexterity; not a humanoid child in costume.
- Exceptional baby fat / plump soft torso and cheeks, very rounded head and soft muzzle, thick fluffy coat, short sturdy legs.
- **Abnormally enormous bear paws**, visibly bigger than twin Millie's; **extra-wide bear tail** rather than long mammal tail; long/big slightly drooping **bear** ears.
- Dark-brown dominant coat on body/back, near-completely **white belly**, cinnamon speckled as fine guard hairs and tonal accent fur around cheeks/muzzle and partly on broad tail.
- **Single deep almost-red cinnamon ear: Acorn's anatomical RIGHT.**
- **Single lighter fur sock: Acorn's anatomical FRONT LEFT paw** (sock shade/height OPEN, *not* automatically white).
- **Hazel eyes**, exact eye shape/iris pattern OPEN.
- No symmetric duplicated red ear or socks; no unapproved patches, shoulder stripes, head markings, or colors.
- Perspective orientation in **nonmirrored straight front view**: viewer's LEFT corresponds to Acorn's RIGHT marked ear; viewer's RIGHT corresponds to his FRONT LEFT sock.

### Scoring state

**Today:** All comparisons **UNTESTED / NOT OBSERVED**: no outputs exist, so no pass/fail grade or overall winner may be reported. This file specifies the future test, not performance outcomes.

## 1. View A — full-body three-quarter reference

**Owner-selected target:** Acorn standing on **all four paws**, body volume, round head, proportions and at least some tail.

**Written composition spec:** Entire body and all four paws should fit in frame with natural ground contact; 3/4 perspective reveals thickness of body and cheek fur, with camera physically plausible at cub scale. Keep body round and low-to-ground, not standing up like a human. A three-quarter angle facing camera should make an effort to distinguish facial structure from belly, though not every colored region is necessarily visible simultaneously.

**Compare:** degree of natural anatomy, fur softness, huge paw proportion, ear droop, broad tail readability, visual cuteness without Disney-style simplification.

**Risk:** One of the two asymmetric markings can be occluded by a 3/4 view; not an automatic failure if correctly established in B/C. A stylized turn shouldn't move them to the wrong side.

## 2. View B — straight-on anatomical reference

**Owner-selected target:** Verify red right ear, front-left sock, fat belly, ears, face and immense paws.

**Written composition spec:** Neutral, symmetrical *camera* facing Acorn, but NOT symmetrical *coat*. Lock the model orientation from Acorn's anatomy: **viewer-left = Acorn-right ear (deep red-cinnamon)**; **viewer-right = Acorn-left forepaw (lighter sock)**. Make his big bear-shaped feet/paws and large slightly drooping bear ears legible. Body stays quadrupedal, not artificially upright solely to show the belly; belly can be indicated from low camera and contour or crosschecked with A/C.

**Compare:** whether asymmetric identity survives the most easily mirrored view; whether one red ear and one sock remain single and on their respective sides; round cheeks, expressive hazel eyes, giant paws.

**Risk:** Frame can hide broad tail. Check tail in view C instead of inventing a longer tail to make it visible.

## 3. View C — side and rear anatomical references

**Owner-selected target:** Broad tail, dark back fur, complete proportions and markings from otherwise obscured angles.

**Written composition spec:** Left profile, right profile and rear directions as *possible subviews* for future documentation; whether to include each exact angle as a separate final image is still open. Use stable lengths, height, shoulder/hip proportions and fur layer. Tail is **extra wide** and cinnamon-tinged in part while remaining bear-like, not elongated like a cat or fox.

**Compare:** bodily continuity; right ear remains on Acorn's right profile, left sock stays on corresponding limb, no swapped marking when poses are mirrored.

**Risk:** White underside is often mostly hidden in rear; don't mistake occlusion for a missing white belly.

## 4. View D — expression reference sheet

**Owner-selected labels:** **Happiness, excitement, anger, embarrassment, sadness and curiosity**.

**Written composition spec:** Head/muzzle/ear close-up variants; no enacted scene, conversation, or event. Natural bear anatomy stays in place while eyes, ear angle, muzzle shape, head tilt and physical posture communicate emotion. Hazel eye color should stay hazel even when lighting and pupil width change. The right special ear stays reddish cinnamon in every variant.

**Compare:** visual distinctiveness without adult human eyebrows, cartoon eyelashes or unnatural huge human irises. Acorn's Q45 eight personality dimensions are broader than six expression labels; the labels do not create mandatory emotional triggers or permanent facial presets.

**Risk:** Altering face shape/size per expression (different cub) rather than acting through consistent anatomy. Embarrassment/anger can look subtle at natural bear scale; study readability without changing species identity.

## 5. View E — streamer webcam close-up

**Owner-selected target:** Acorn at gaming setup with convincing cub-scale gear and camera.

**Written composition spec:** A seated/crouching/lying position may later be chosen to be ergonomically compatible with a quadrupedal bear, but no specific pose or equipment brand has been approved. The webcam crop prioritizes chubby face and hazel eyes while leaving enough fur/ear visibility for identity. A camera can exclude the broad tail/sock from the crop while a fuller reference (A/B/C) proves the physical traits.

**Compare:** whether Acorn reads as **actually present at a functional bear-operated gaming station**, not a floating cub head pasted onto a human desktop. His normal voice is tiny/high-pitched if audio is ever later separately produced, not a gruff adult masculine voice.

**Game-screen context:** Use the user's favored real games as future candidate background/application subject, rather than random fabricated games. See [Q56 preference-based pool](ACORN_GAME_REFERENCE_POOL_Q56_v0.1.md). **Fortnite already explicitly belongs to Acorn's canon**; no other title is automatically an established Acorn play session.

**Risk:** Mistaking optional bear-operator accessibility devices for approved exact technology; a technical gameplay interface remains open.

## 6. View F — complete livestream screenshot composition

**Owner-selected target:** A real-game footage *area*, Acorn webcam, chat and overlay visually functioning as one believable broadcast.

**Written composition spec (suggestion, not a fixed approved layout):**
- **Primary content zone:** genuine game UI/action, properly legible, not AI-produced fake game title text.
- **Acorn webcam zone:** enough body/face to communicate reactions while preserving the actual-game content.
- **Chat zone:** consistent readable messages and a believable density scale; don't claim Acorn reads all comments manually (he uses TTS).
- **Optional overlay/status elements:** donation or moderation notices only when present by future scenario choice; no forced paid-feed requirements, sponsor logos, fixed platform branding or invented channel handle.
- **Bear-scale interface:** Acorn not forced into human keyboards/hands. A proposed layout is not canon game UI or an enacted stream.

**Comparison game types:** Fortnite first as established Acorn game; additional *user-liked candidate titles* can test sandbox/strategy/story, creature or cozy contexts. See the pool. Do not confuse comparison candidates with completed streams.

**Compare:** Acorn visible vs game readable vs chat legible, same aesthetic family, no fake-gibberish text/uncanny game logo/sponsor clutter, adult-sounding UI labels assigned to Acorn incorrectly.

**Risk:** The “game screenshot” reads as generated AI slop—nonsense fake chat, impossible physical setup, mismatched UI scale and irrelevant tiny bear. These are future checks, not a claim that a visual was actually tested.

## 7. View G — indoor and outdoor full-body contexts

**Owner-selected target:** Acorn legible in **an indoor space and a natural environment**; **don't invent specific HoneyDweller estate locations**.

**Written composition spec:** The character remains consistent in fur color, lighting and scale while changing from a generic interior-lighting condition to natural light. Be careful that very dark brown body doesn't disappear in dim lighting and white belly doesn't become tan in warm light. Natural surroundings cannot make his tail grow or muzzle/eye shape change.

**Compare:** environmental contact, paws grounded, belly/fur silhouettes, broad tail, ear and sock color recognition, consistent anatomy under different lighting.

**Risk:** The character becomes a different cub due to changing style/perspective or exposure. No outdoor adventure event, family outing or real-life geography is canonized.

## 8. View H — family comparative scale

**Owner-selected target:** Acorn beside twin sister **Millie** or an adult bear, with unapproved family details provisional.

**Written composition spec:** Show **Acorn's** approved proportions clearly; for Millie, the only directly specified relative morphological comparison is that her **paws are smaller than Acorn's**. Her coat color, eye color, absolute body size, height and other anatomy are **not** decided. An adult bear's identity and proportions are also not automatically canonized by this reference view. A future art brief could use a labeled **generic scale placeholder** rather than pretending to show finalized Millie/Audrey/Rosa.

**Compare:** believable cub developmental stage and relative scale; preserve Acorn's individuality without treating Millie as a recolored identical model; do not assign her Q48–55 color features.

**Risk:** A fabricated sister character design spreads into canon; mark placeholders unmistakably provisional in future art directions.

## 10. Fair comparison and acceptance checklist

- All eight Q56 views are in-scope; no single generated image must somehow show every view simultaneously.
- Q54 fidelity gate runs **before** beauty or aesthetic preference; identity errors are **FAIL**, not a weighted tradeoff.
- Marking orientation: **RIGHT ear** red-cinnamon, **FRONT LEFT sock** lighter, both anatomically correct across frames.
- Owner-approved body: very fat soft infant bear, not a lean adult or bipedal cartoon.
- Coat: very DARK back/body, almost all-WHITE belly, selective cinnamon guard hairs/face/tail.
- View D: exactly six user-listed emotion labels as expression *study goals*, while retaining natural expression cues. No scene-writing or dialogue.
- Views E/F: genuine existing preferred game subjects, with **Fortnite canon** and other titles **candidate only**. Do not generate bogus screenshots of products, mislabel one game as another, or create official-looking game branding without authorization.
- View G: no invented new estate locations or outings.
- View H: family size comparison doesn't authorize Millie's full physical design.
- **Not observed:** No render, screenshot, physical playtest, character sheet image or code exists from this decision, so no actual PASS/FAIL claims.
