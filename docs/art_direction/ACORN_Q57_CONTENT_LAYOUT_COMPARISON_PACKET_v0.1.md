## 1. General UI design requirements across reference categories

The user wants Acorn to feel like a genuine variety streamer, not a generic fictional baby-bear gamer with nonsensical mock HUDs. **Use actual game titles the user chose as written reference contexts** while making any future concrete on-screen captures, licensing, in-world availability and streaming-tech decisions separate.

- **Primary content must be identifiable:** gaming interface, Just Chatting reaction content, fanfiction excerpt layout, or music-video discussion each require different screen priorities. A one-size overlay does not suit all Q57 selections.
- **Acorn must remain recognizable:** Six-month brown-bear cub, very chubby/round/fluffy, dark brown body and back, almost-white belly, cinnamon guard hairs/face/tail, large slightly drooping ears, extra-wide tail, enormous paws, hazel eyes, **RIGHT** special reddish-cinnamon ear and **FRONT LEFT** lighter sock. Camera occlusion is okay, mirroring his anatomy is not.
- **Bear-operated setup:** A believable cub-scale station must fit a low, four-legged baby bear. The user has not selected specific paw controllers, game hardware, webcam mounts, chairs or number of screens. Staff assistance is possible under existing canon but not compulsory in every hypothetical layout.
- **Streaming UI readability:** legible high-contrast text and chat placement with enough room for game footage. No gibberish UI text, arbitrary sponsor logos, overfull overlays, fake or invented real-game HUDs, unrealistic viewer counts or fabricated stream titles.
- **Dynamic format:** Q47 approved Acorn chooses what to stream. Written layouts can have *optional modes* for different content, not mandatory segments or a fixed weekly schedule.
- **Audience agency limits:** Chat votes/donations do not force eating, physical danger, mature content exposure or surrender of channel control. He can change his mind.
- **Content caution:** The user likes adult/mature game media and edgy humor. Q57 approves reference subjects, not specific mature scenes or a toddler cub viewing pornography. What can actually appear on a fictional platform remains OPEN.
- **Music/other content rights:** No actual audio, video clips, verbatim song lyrics, author-owned fanfiction text, platform logos or copyrighted game HUD art copied in this design document.

## 3. Non-game reaction modes from Q57 custom input

### Fanfiction — reference category approved
**UI objective:** Distinguish a readable textual reading/commentary area, Acorn camera and reactions, chat comments/TTS. The specific fandom, author, text, age rating and permission are **OPEN**. Do not generate or quote fictional fanfic prose as if it were part of a stream; this is a layout exercise only.

### Edgy / surprising internet humor — reference category approved
**UI objective:** Distinguish reaction content and Acorn's commentary without converting his personality into adult speech. The user wants boundary-pushing internet culture in a comic/satirical stream universe, but no specific site/clip has been chosen.

### Orange-and-black-logo website — *reference unclear*
User gave the phrase “a orange and black logo website” alongside “funny like non-appropirate stuff.” It may allude to an adult-oriented brand; **do not canonize a particular URL, logo or actual website**. Its potential use in a non-explicit satire/commentary brief must be distinguished from exposing a developmentally toddler-like character to pornographic media. **No explicit adult sexual content is authorized or proposed for Acorn**.

### Rapper music videos — reference category approved
**UI objective:** Written layout concept for media-player/reaction content and discussion with Acorn's webcam/chat integrated. **No artist, song, lyrics, clip, endorsement, story interaction or musical playback** chosen. Copyright and fictional in-world media rules remain undecided. Image-generation is not authorized.

## 4. Potential adaptive stream layout modes — PROPOSALS, not Q57 approvals

1. **Gameplay-first:** Main real-game viewport large; chat and small-but-readable Acorn webcam at edge.
2. **Reaction-first:** Larger Acorn camera plus a clip/text panel and legible chat; suitable for fanfiction, public clips or music-video commentary if content later approved.
3. **Just Chatting-first:** Acorn camera dominates, chat accessible; can accommodate Q47 questions, stories, rants or toys.
4. **Split-screen collaboration:** Multiple bear participants or family cameras only if they are approved and actually taking part in a later planned stream; family appearances beyond Acorn remain provisional.
5. **Outdoor/cub activity:** The character camera is main content; equipment and platform overlay remain unobtrusive.
6. **Contextual UI reduction:** TTS/donation/alerts appear when relevant, not constant clutter. Do not invent obligatory donations or sponsor strips.

These six proposals should lead into **Q58 interface/format interview** with multi-select and Other/custom. **The user has NOT yet chosen these modes** as canonical layouts.
