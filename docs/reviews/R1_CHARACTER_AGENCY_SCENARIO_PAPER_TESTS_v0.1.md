# R1 — Character/Family Agency Paper Tests v0.1

**Date:** 2026-11-08  
**Status:** Documentation-only consistency evaluation of [R1 character, routine and authority spec](../character/ACORN_R1_CHARACTER_ROUTINE_FAMILY_AGENCY_SYSTEM_v0.1.md).  
**Not** enacted scenes or approved history. Counterfactual inputs, response examples and test verdicts are **logical checks against known facts**, not fiction that has happened. No code, gameplay, dialogue, decisions made for the owner, audio/art or fictional time advancement.

## Test method

For each hypothetical:
- **Grounded inputs:** facts the existing user canon has actually established.
- **Permissible outcomes:** more than one plausible conditional reaction; these are **examples only** unless already grounded.
- **Reject:** outcomes requiring a new unapproved fact, knowledge leak, age-inappropriate mind/hands, or erasure of approved independence.
- **Verdict:** **PASS (paper consistency)** means coherent noncanonical outcomes can be constructed from approved facts. **OPEN** means a rule/physical assumption must be selected before a determinate answer is possible. A paper test passing does **not** claim a runtime test, safety certification or a canonical story scene.
