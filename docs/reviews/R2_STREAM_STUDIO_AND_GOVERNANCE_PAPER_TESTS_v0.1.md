## Test verdict definitions

- **PASS (PAPER)**: A coherent design path exists preserving approved constraints; it does NOT establish real physical feasibility or user approval of the suggested option.
- **CONDITIONAL**: One or more suggested components may work in fiction if later approved and specified; no certified operation.
- **OPEN/BLOCKING FOR FINAL CANON**: A meaningful owner decision, story world law, safety or physical feasibility gap remains. **This does NOT block documenting the proposal.**
- **FAIL**: A proposal would contradict established user canon; must be rejected or revised before implementation.

## Adversarial examples that would FAIL approved canon

**F1: “Mommy owns the channel because a toddler cannot.”** Contradicts ordinary Acorn ownership/direct control. No unchosen bear-legal arrangement can silently erase this.

**F2: “A staff member uses a real controller while Acorn pretends to play.”** Contradicts genuine Fortnite ability and player-driven skill growth.

**F3: “The middle-left broadcast webcam must be physically mounted to the left bedroom wall.”** Confuses video composition with physical placement and contradicts the unchosen camera geometry.

**F4: “Ten Q57 research references means ten actual prior Acorn gameplay sessions.”** Incorrectly manufactures streamer history.

**F5: “Chat has a giant visible queue because backend needs one.”** Q61 C is **unselected**; hidden processing is possible, not an approved on-screen queue.

**F6: “Since Mama is a parent she can permanently revoke his bans and stream every time she is annoyed.”** Not approved; overextends caregiver access into ordinary editor control.

**F7: “Because parents can rescue in an emergency, they already know about H2.”** Directly conflicts with approved parental ignorance.

**F8: “We need an enormous keyboard and an adult chair.”** Humanized body/control trope unsupported by cub naturalistic anatomy.

**F9: “Acorn can stream on current literal Twitch because a six-month bear is human-age two.”** Human developmental analogy does NOT waive Twitch min-age terms.

**F10: “Acorn's Twitch/Epic personal accounts are already installed, verified or legally valid.”** No external service connection/setup occurred.

## Cross-cutting invariant checklist

- **C01** No software, livestream or playable input built.
- **C02** The user has not approved a particular real-world controller/brand or physical game-room layout.
- **C03** Acorn's genuine input and gameplay errors drive progression, not an AI/staff takeover.
- **C04** Sensory considerations, baby emotion and unusually advanced reading coexist.
- **C05** Ordinary channel authority retained; safety/care rules unselected.
- **C06** No schedule, new stream incident, viewer identities, sponsorship or monetization invented.
- **C07** On-screen middle-left webcam + normal below-chat preserved; TTS beside camera, pause/skip/resume; **no visible queue approval**.
- **C08** Current real Twitch age requirements explicitly separate from fictional bear-world platform decisions.
- **C09** Epic younger-player support for Fortnite doesn't imply stream-platform approval.
- **C10** No reference title beyond Fortnite reclassified as already played.
- **C11** H1 and H2 distinguished; both mothers unaware of H2, but know H1; Q73 tight gap only to GET UNDER bed, floor hatch not cramped.
- **C12** Rudy's polar-bear girlfriend unnamed; Mama/Mommy/Cynthia first names remain working where applicable.

## Summary results

**22 paper cases analyzed**. They expose **no forced retcon of direct user-approved canon** in the presented design choices. Several PASS reasoning paths exist; significant **OPEN BLOCKERS** remain for *finalized* physical gameplay (two-axis plus action ergonomics), the bear-internet Fortnite platform, caregiver interruption, staff authority and account legality. The documentation packet **is complete as a design draft**, not a tested real controller, child safety certification or ratified world law.

**Recommended high-impact next choice (not yet canon):** bundled platform model (F1/F2/F3), game input family (P1/P2/P3), and care/stream intervention level (G1/G2/G3), with Other/custom. A separate next workstream R3 may document viewer/staff roles at proposal level without assuming any of these choices have been made.
