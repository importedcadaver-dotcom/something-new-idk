## 1. Executive verdict and priorities

**Verdict: No demonstrated mutually exclusive pair of direct user-approved story facts requiring a retcon.** The family genealogy and the corporate transfer are compatible **if** unknown actual ages, biology and chronology can be filled in consistently. This is a **conditional compatibility finding**, not proof every imagined implementation works.

**Highest severity risk (P0 — explanation needed before final high-realism world bible):** **H2** is a luxury, excavated, multi-zone basement under Acorn's bedroom floor, built mostly by high-school teenage Rudy and his polar girlfriend with a baby cub participating, unknown to two attentive mothers in an extensively staffed, ultrawealthy estate. No approved explanation yet accounts for the **time, material/soil movement, noise/vibration, workers, access, structural integrity, services, ongoing inspections, emergency rescue and the cub's passage through the cramped exterior under-bed gap**. Fictionally accepted existence should be retained, but its day-to-day realism cannot be declared solved.

**Second severity risk (P1):** Family age progression and succession **need an explicit adult/child development curve**: 4× human developmental equivalent applies to early development, not an indefinite 4× multiplier across a 120-year life. Rudy's exact bear age, the mothers' age now and at T−17, gestational/marital details, and Cynthia's prior crime timeline remain open.

**Third severity risk (P1):** Knowledge boundaries. Cynthia's murder order is approved but *not* known by the mothers; neither mother knows secret H2; H1 is a separate parent-known bookshelf retreat. No plausible plot can rely on a character's omniscience.

**R4 paper exit:** The contradictions are categorized without retcons; the next final-unified-bible milestone can retain explicit open fields. R4 does **not** authorize a new chronological first-stream history.
