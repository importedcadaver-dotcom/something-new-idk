## Highest priority — approved

**“Anatomy and character fidelity”** is the most important evaluative consideration. Interpretation: **fail any proposed direction that cannot preserve the approved anatomy and unique Acorn identity** before comparing its attractiveness, emotional range, screenshot believability or scalability. This is not a numeric percentage, not a weighted-average score, and not a selection of a final art style.

- **Anatomy requirement:** recognizably six-month-old, naturalistic mostly quadrupedal brown-bear physiology; plump infant build; large slightly drooping bear ears, giant anatomically correct paws, broad bear tail and thick fur; no humanized fingers/body.
- **Character requirement:** primarily dark-brown body/back; nearly all-white belly; cinnamon dispersed as guard hairs, cheek/muzzle accents and some tail fur; **exactly one** deep near-red cinnamon ear whose left/right side remains UNDECIDED; **lighter sock on Acorn's own FRONT LEFT**; hazel eyes.
- **Do not** confuse a more photorealistic render with automatically higher fidelity: a 2D/pixel solution can satisfy the anatomy/identity gate through deliberate simplification, even where eyelashes or individual guard hairs cannot be represented at small scale. Conversely, an extremely photorealistic but incorrectly proportioned bear fails.
