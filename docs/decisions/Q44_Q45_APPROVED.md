# Approved decisions Q44–Q45 — Bear Cub Streamer
**Logged:** 2026-11-07  
**Status:** Q44 physical-development direction APPROVED; Q45 personality traits A–H APPROVED. No gameplay, scenes, code, final art, or fictional time advancement.

## Q44 — physical development: APPROVED E
**User selection:** “E. Hybrid — natural early growth, adjustable later biology. Keep realistic-looking cubs and mostly natural early physical development, but design puberty, growth completion, nursing and longevity independently.”

**Implications directly approved:**
- Realistic-looking cubs and broadly natural early physical development as baseline.
- Later biological systems may be modeled independently, without forcing puberty, growth completion, nursing and longevity to share one schedule.
- Protected world rules persist: **one actual bear year ≈ four human developmental years during development**, **80–120 actual-year lifespan**, **naturalistic/quadrupedal bear anatomy with enhanced dexterity**.

**NOT approved by Q44:** Numerical physical-growth stage cutoffs from proposal v0.1, puberty age, nursing/weaning duration, reproductive timing, when adult body size is reached, mental maturity plateau, elderly threshold, legal ages, individual character ages. Option D species-based growth variation was previously *recommended* as a supplement, **not selected in Q44**. The prior research proposal remains a proposal with this approved choice overriding it where applicable.

## Q45 — Acorn's on-stream personality: APPROVED A+B+C+D+E+F+G+H

The user explicitly selected **“all of them!!!!”** in response to eight independent possible character traits; all are approved as **coexisting, context-dependent dimensions** rather than mutually exclusive static modes.

| Letter | Approved dimension | Observable characterization without invented events |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Chaotic toddler gremlin | Loud, impulsive, demanding, funny, dramatic, frequent small disasters |
| **B** | Sweet baby entertainer | Adorable, affectionate, funny, seeks to please and display things to his audience |
| **C** | Competitive little gamer | Invested in games, celebrates wins, can melt down after losses, seeks praise for progress |
| **D** | Sensitive emotional streamer | Reacts strongly, easily cries or becomes embarrassed, seeks reassurance; viewers may feel protective |
| **E** | Tiny rich brat celebrity | Bossy, entitled, expects obedience, humor rooted in extravagant privilege |
| **F** | Curious talkative host | Narrates, asks questions, comments on surroundings and talks to chat a great deal |
| **G** | Performance switch | Shy and restrained initially/when insecure; expressive and chaotic when comfortable |
| **H** | Family-content magnet | Family/pet dynamics and interruptions contribute heavily to audience interest |

**Interaction rules / canon protections:** No trait erases another. He can be sweet and bratty, competitive and fragile, shy with unfamiliar bears and wild with familiar people. His advanced early reading, gamer vocabulary and real gaming do not equal adult emotional maturity or adult diction. Acorn remains a **six-month-old male brown bear** (about human developmental age two), and his normal speech is toddler-like. Ownership and ordinary control of his channel persist; the other main family members' approved independent personalities persist.

**NOT decided by Q45:** Exact voice timbre, accent, pitch, pronunciation, vocal sound design, sample dialogue, streaming schedule/format, visual reference, appearance beyond brown bear juvenile morphology, art style, drawings, channel handle, platform, viewer identities or specific scripted incidents. All such content needs later explicit approval, separate from current no-scenes/no-art-yet directive.
