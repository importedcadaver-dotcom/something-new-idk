## Canonical character reference across all candidates

Use the consolidated [Acorn Visual Canon Sheet v0.2](../ACORN_VISUAL_CANON_SHEET_v0.2.md) and Q48–52 direct approvals. Across every style, preserve:

- Six-month-old **male brown bear** with naturalistic, largely quadrupedal anatomy (no human hands, humanized face or upright mascot body).
- Very **plump, fat, well fed**, short and stout, unusually thick fluffy coat, big soft rounded cheeks/face.
- **Abnormally oversized bear paws**—larger than twin sister Millie's—and an **extra-wide tail**.
- **Very large/relatively long bear ears** that droop somewhat.
- Hazel irises with especially expressive but bear-anatomical eyes/ears; exact eye shape **OPEN**.
- Dark brown dominant body/back; warm cinnamon highlights as **scattered guard hairs**, around cheeks/muzzle, and on some fur of broad tail.
- Belly **almost entirely white**, not tan or beige.
- **One** deep almost-red cinnamon ear, but **WHICH EAR (Acorn's left/right) IS STILL OPEN**. Do not quietly canonize either side.
- A **lighter sock on his anatomical FRONT-LEFT paw**. Exact hue and sock height **OPEN**; do not assume white or mirror to the front-right.

Ear side, exact sock hue/height, detailed facial geometry/iris color distribution, coat RGB palette, art production medium, pixel dimensions, 3D polygon/texture budgets, camera, streamer overlay UI and image-generation prompts are **not** approved by Q53.

## Design deliverable: side-by-side *briefs*, not pictures

See [Six Art Directions — Comparative Brief and Acceptance Tests](../art_direction/ACORN_STYLE_COMPARISON_Q53_v0.1.md), which describes A through F using parallel criteria. It is a **PROPOSED design analysis**, with no adopted favorite.

The study must evaluate at least:
1. Species fidelity and 6-month baby-bear body proportions;
2. Visibility of plump face, giant paws, big drooping ears, broad tail and white belly;
3. Distinguishable front-left sock and one red-cinnamon ear, with ear side unresolved;
4. Hazel eye and cinnamon guard-hair legibility;
5. Warm/cute but distinctive expression without generic art;
6. Suitability for live webcam, reaction videos, Fortnite screen or outdoor streaming presentations;
7. Consistency across front, three-quarter, side/rear and close-up views;
8. Production/animation burden, and art style consistency across later characters/UI.

**No definitive winner** can be reported without a future owner choice. The Q53 comparison is useful even before images are authorized.

## What is specifically NOT approved

- No art/image generation, image edits, gameplay mockups, actual stream UI renders or screenshot production.
- No final choice among A–F; no specific brand-game imitation or copyrighted style/character cloning.
- No assignment of reddish-cinnamon ear to left or right; no specified eye shape, sock color or extra signature patch.
- No camera projection, sprite size, 3D implementation engine or real app.
- No game format changes: world bible first and interactive experience later.
