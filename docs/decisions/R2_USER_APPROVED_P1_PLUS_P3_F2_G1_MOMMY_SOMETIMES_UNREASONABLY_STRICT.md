## 3. Caregiver intervention — G1 (APPROVED)

**G1 original offered wording:** “Acorn-first: He controls ordinary streams; parents negotiate routine care but can intervene proportionately for serious danger.”  
**Binding design and hierarchy:**
1. **Ordinary broadcast choices** remain Acorn's: go-live/end, games/content, guests (including asking Mommy to leave ordinary stream participation), bans/unbans, donations/food/challenge refusals, chat/TTS skip/pause/resume. Staff assist and filter per already approved Q61 capabilities.

**Not selected:** G2 prearranged cooperative family-care windows as a binding routine, G3 broadly empowered parent pauses for ordinary care, a formal automated parent kill switch, indefinite parental administrative takeover, “parents have no rights in emergency,” or legal/financial child-account signing rules.
